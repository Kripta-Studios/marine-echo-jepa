# Meeting brief — 29 September 2026

**Question:** Can a history of acoustic observations help anticipate the next useful signal, and tell an operator when the available information is too weak or stale to support a decision?

We have completed research software and bounded public-data studies. Marine operational value remains unvalidated. The immediate demonstration uses the exact independently approved [AEON r3 offline package](../../release/aeon-offline-scale-expanded-20260928-r3.zip), not a new experiment or Marine integration.

The application presents saved forecasts, quantile uncertainty, comparison tables, study limits and provenance. Its AEON replay serves historical predictions at +1/+3/+6 **source intervals**, not checkpoint inference. It does not return later truth. The [static fallback](../../evidence/meeting-readiness-20260929/static-fallback.html) shows the two predetermined replay cutoffs and already released aggregate evidence; it supplies no invented observation reveal. A separate first chronological packaged MOSAiC response-code example supplies later observations (+1 missing, +3/+6 present), labelled uncalibrated engineering evidence, never AEON or JEPA truth.

The AEON target is publisher-conditioned 38 kHz FullDepth `Sv_mean`, in dB re 1 m^-1, from a public research deployment. Four-frequency histories and observation masks support forecasts. Source timezone, operational availability, conditioning and calibration lineage remain unresolved. This is neither a depth-resolved Marine product nor a tuna, biomass, catch or fuel-savings model.

Results are mixed. In the original one-deployment, non-sealed retrospective comparison, EMA-JEPA pinball was **0.533954 dB**, versus direct **0.563387 dB**: a positive result within that frozen study. The original frozen models' descriptive same-site prior-year transfer was negative: EMA **0.678922**, direct **0.670178**. The primary cross-site comparison was metadata-ineligible because a 0–230 m aggregate cannot be cropped to 0–200 m without more information. That prior-year cohort later became TRAIN for expanded models and affected descendants; it is not their external test.

The completed [checkpoint diagnostic](../../orchestration/reports/AEON_CHECKPOINT_OBJECTIVE_DIAGNOSTIC_20260929.md) adds exposed development evidence outside r3. Equal-three-seed prediction ensembles score **0.639577** for direct 1500, **0.639062** for direct 3000 and **0.642170** for EMA. Direct 1500 beats EMA per seed and ensemble; each direct seed worsens at 3000 while its ensemble improves. These are scores of mean predictions, not means of scores, prospective selection or new significance. Gradient restrictions and scale mismatch are established; causal attribution remains unresolved. JEPA is a tested candidate, never a guaranteed winning method.
